**The cybernetics market** does not have a distinct, widely tracked standalone market size, as it is a foundational interdisciplinary field (focused on control, feedback, and communication systems) rather than a product category. Its principles are embedded in robotics, industrial automation, control systems, and related sectors.

**The robotics market** is the primary commercial embodiment and is experiencing strong growth, driven by automation, AI integration, labor shortages, manufacturing reshoring, and new applications (e.g., service, medical, humanoid, and logistics robots).

### Current Robotics Market Size (as of 2025–2026)
Estimates vary by scope (industrial-only vs. total robotics including service, professional, and consumer):

- **Total Global Robotics Market**: Roughly **USD 50–88 billion** in 2025/2026.
  - One source: ~USD 50 billion in 2025.
  - Broader estimates: USD 53–73 billion in 2025, rising to ~USD 59–88 billion in 2026.

- **Industrial Robotics** (core segment, e.g., factory arms): **USD 27–54 billion** in 2025/2026.

- **Service Robotics**: Growing rapidly, with estimates in the USD 26–68 billion range for recent years, depending on inclusion of professional vs. personal robots.

### Projections
Projections show robust expansion through 2030–2035, with CAGRs typically in the 12–19% range (higher for service/intelligent/AI-driven segments).

| Segment                  | Recent (2025/2026)     | Mid-Term (2030–2031)     | Longer-Term (2035)       | Approx. CAGR          |
|--------------------------|------------------------|--------------------------|--------------------------|-----------------------|
| **Total Robotics**      | ~USD 50–88B           | USD 110–220B+           | USD 200–476B            | 14–19%               |
| **Industrial Robotics** | ~USD 27–54B           | USD 60–94B              | USD 77–93B+             | 10–15%               |
| **Service Robotics**    | ~USD 26–68B           | USD 100–210B            | USD 130–234B            | 14–20%               |

**Key drivers** of this growth include AI embodiment, collaborative robots (cobots), humanoid development, e-commerce/logistics automation, healthcare/surgical applications, and defense/exploration uses.

**Related broader markets** (where cybernetics/control principles are central):
- Industrial automation and control systems: ~USD 209 billion in 2026, potentially reaching ~USD 390 billion by 2033.
- Specific niches like soft robotics or intelligent robotics show even higher growth rates (20–30%+ CAGR in some forecasts).

### Caveats
- Figures differ across research firms (e.g., ABI Research, Grand View, Mordor, Precedence, Fortune) due to varying definitions of “robotics” (hardware-only vs. including software/services) and inclusion of adjacent automation.
- Growth is not uniform: Asia-Pacific (especially China, Japan, South Korea) leads in volume, while North America and Europe emphasize high-value, AI-integrated systems.
- Risks include supply chain issues, high initial costs, skills gaps, and economic cycles, but tailwinds like labor shortages and AI advances are dominant.

In short, the **robotics market is currently worth around USD 50–80+ billion** and is on track to **more than double or triple by the early 2030s**, potentially reaching **hundreds of billions by 2035**. This makes it one of the stronger high-tech growth sectors. Cybernetics contributes foundational value across these markets through advanced control and adaptive systems.

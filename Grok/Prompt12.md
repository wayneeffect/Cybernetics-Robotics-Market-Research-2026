**Tips for choosing**:
- For **industrial automation/controls/robot programming** — Prioritize DAVRON, Sterling, or SCM Talent Group.
- For **advanced/AI-integrated/senior robotics roles** — Blue Signal, Alpha Apex, Direct Recruiters, or Protingent offer deeper networks.
- Robotics talent is scarce and often requires specific hardware/software experience (ROS, Python/C++, computer vision, sensors, etc.). Look for agencies with technical vetting, industry knowledge, and placement guarantees.
- Many roles involve manufacturing, automotive, logistics, healthcare (surgical robotics), or defense. Specify your sub-sector for better matches.
- Check reviews, retention rates, and time-to-fill on sites like Clutch or Glassdoor.

## Market Overview (2026)

The global **robotics market** is currently valued between **USD 50–88 billion** and is experiencing strong double-digit growth. 

- **Projections**: Expected to reach **$110–220 billion by 2030–2031** and potentially **$200–476 billion by 2035** (CAGR 14–19%).
- **Key Drivers**: AI embodiment, labor shortages, manufacturing reshoring, logistics automation, humanoid robots, and medical/service robotics.
- **Talent Demand**: Persistent skills gap — companies actively seek engineers who can translate simulation into reliable real-world systems.

Cybernetics principles (feedback loops, adaptive control, communication, and self-regulation) remain foundational to modern robotics, especially in **controls engineering**, autonomy, and human-robot interaction.
